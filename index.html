About Me

I'm a frontend engineer and UI/UX designer who likes building interfaces that feel a little alive — small details, real motion, and enough polish that people notice without being told to look for it.

Most of my work sits at the edge of engineering and design: prototyping in code, sweating the interaction details, and shipping things that hold up outside of a demo. This site — a fully simulated aquarium running behind every page — is a good example of the kind of over-engineered detail I enjoy way too much.
